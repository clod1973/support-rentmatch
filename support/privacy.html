Privacy Policy – Rent & Buy Match

Last updated: December 2025

Information We Collect

Rent & Buy Match collects only the information required to operate the app and provide the lead-matching service. This includes your name, contact details, housing preferences, selected locations, and app activity. Payment information is processed securely by Apple and is never stored by us.

How We Use Your Data

  • Creating and managing your account
  • Matching your rental or purchase preferences with available property options
  • Improving the functionality, performance, and security of the app

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage & Security

All data is encrypted, stored securely, and handled in accordance with GDPR and applicable privacy regulations. Only authorized systems that support the Rent & Buy Match app can access your information.
